## Manual QA (2026-09-24; polish round after the QA report)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack. The
  authoring sources assemble into `tools/passages_src.json` (`"src": "gen"`);
  the assembler cycles each mc key through positions 0-3. The builder writes
  `pack/passages.json` and this report. Two runs give byte-identical output.
- **Builder.** vocab-engine packbuilder plus passage-only Indonesian hooks in
  `langs/id.py` (`passage_text`, `passage_retag`, `passage_post_resolve`,
  `passage_fallback_ok`, `passage_phrase_ranges`, `passage_names_never_link`).
  The word and sentence build never call these hooks. `passages.py` has two
  additive changes: the opt-in `passage_names_never_link` flag (id only) and
  the report-only title/option note above.
  - Address forms: Bu, Kak, Dik, Dok anywhere, and Nenek, Kakek, Ayah, Mama
    capitalised mid-sentence, link the pack noun (ibu, kakak, adik, dokter,
    nenek...), as the build does for Pak/Ibu/Paman. Title nouns capitalised
    mid-sentence (Ketua in "Yth. Bapak Ketua RT", Manajer) link the noun.
  - PROPN: over all passage texts, questions and options, 0 tokens tagged
    PROPN are spelled like a pack word, declared names aside.
  - Verb forms the tagger leaves unresolved or mislemmatises link the pack
    verb they are built on: object-voice -i (hubungi, sukai, pelajari),
    me-/di- forms (memarkir -> parkir, menunjuk -> tunjuk), a di- form whose
    tagger lemma is a pack non-verb (dikurangi -> mengurangi), and a di- or
    bare -kan/-i form whose me- form is a pack verb (dikembalikan ->
    mengembalikan, not kembali; masukkan -> memasukkan).
  - One tap per compound (`PASSAGE_COMPOUNDS`): one span over both words.
    A compound that is a pack word links it (beri tahu, memberi tahu ->
    memberitahu). A compound the pack lacks links its first word (orang tua,
    rumah sakit, rumah makan, kamar mandi/tidur, tempat tidur, ruang
    tamu/tunggu, air minum, kereta api, masa depan, salah satu, ulang tahun,
    tata bahasa, tentu saja, makan siang/malam, teman kerja, mabuk laut, masuk
    akal, sepak bola, buku tulis); its second word never links alone. All 10
    uses of orang tua are one span; old people in p0038 is now "orang yang
    sudah tua". luar negeri (p0043) stays two links (outside + country).
  - Quotative katanya (after a closing quote or opening a clause) links
    berkata, not kata "word" (6 uses). baru before saja/akan or a verb,
    after a non-noun, is the adverb "just", except "yang baru ada/adalah"
    (p0004: new). A verb/noun homograph opening a clause (Ternyata hidup...,
    bahwa hidup...) and a verb after cara (cara bicara) link the noun.
  - A declared name never links and is not counted (Jawa Tengah, Museum
    Nasional, Selatan, Cerdas, Mawar, Padang). Month names are not declared,
    so Agustus and Mei link like the other months. Abbreviations Yth, SD and
    RT are declared names.
  - On the 493 sentences whose text did not change in the polish round, 55
    changed links, each from one of the rules above.
